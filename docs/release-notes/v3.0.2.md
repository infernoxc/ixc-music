**IXC Music is now part of IXC 3**: music, chat (Twitch, Kick, YouTube, Rumble), chat voice, overlays and a phone remote in
one program for OBS. This is the same release as [IXC v3.0.2 in infernoxc/ixc-chatbox](https://github.com/infernoxc/ixc-chatbox/releases/tag/v3.0.2).

### Install or update
1. Close OBS.
2. Download **`IXC-Setup-v3.0.2.exe`** below and run it. Choose **Express**.
3. Open OBS. The **IXC Music**, **IXC ChatBox** and **IXC** panels are under **Docks**.

Coming from IXC Music 2.x: just run the setup. Your queue, settings and Spotify keys are kept (and backed up first).

### What's new for music
- The queue is kept by IXC: restarting OBS, IXC or the PC loses nothing.
- Your own music folders, next to YouTube and Spotify links.
- Song requests from chat (`!sr`, `!song`) with permissions, cooldowns and limits; `!wrongsong`.
- Chat voice ducking: the music fades down while the voice speaks and always comes back.
- Now Playing overlays and text files; **Music goes to ALL / TWITCH / KICK / YOUTUBE** as before.
- Phone remote with no setup: **Show QR code** and scan it.

Full notes, step-by-step guide and known limitations: [infernoxc/ixc-chatbox](https://github.com/infernoxc/ixc-chatbox/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md).
