**IXC Music is part of IXC** (music, chat, chat voice, overlays and a phone remote in one program for OBS). This is the same release
as [IXC v3.2.0 in infernoxc/ixc-chatbox](https://github.com/infernoxc/ixc-chatbox/releases/tag/v3.2.0).

## ⬇ How to install
1. Install **OBS Studio 30 or newer** ([obsproject.com](https://obsproject.com)) and open it once.
2. Below, under **Assets**, click **`IXC-Setup-v3.2.0.exe`** to download it.
3. Double-click it in your **Downloads** folder. If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**: **More info** > **Run anyway**.
4. Click **Express setup** (close OBS if the setup asks), then follow IXC's short setup assistant.
5. Open **OBS**: the **IXC Music**, **IXC ChatBox** and **IXC** panels are under **Docks**.

Already have IXC or IXC Music 2.x? Just run the setup: your queue, playlists and settings are kept (and backed up first).

## New for music in 3.2.0
- **No more repeating songs**: Shuffle plays every song once before any song repeats; Autoplay skips songs you just heard,
  also other uploads of them.
- **Playlists**: save the queue, play or add it later. **Recently played** list.
- **Audio routing**: switch the music (and the chat voice) on or off per platform, e.g. music on Twitch and YouTube but not
  on Kick. The music dock and the phone remote have the same switches. Needs one OBS output per platform (multistream).
- **Starts and stops with OBS**: IXC opens when OBS opens and closes when OBS closes.

Full details: [IXC 3.2.0 release notes](https://github.com/infernoxc/ixc-chatbox/releases/tag/v3.2.0).
